Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about booking a scooty, bike, car or EV? Find instant answers to all common queries below.

search

assignment_ind Booking, Documents & Digital KYC

What documents are required to rent a vehicle with DriveX? expand_more
You need a valid original Driving Licence (appropriate for the vehicle category) and Aadhaar Card. Our 2-minute digital KYC validates your documents paperlessly via DigiLocker / OCR without storing confidential login credentials.
What is the minimum age to book a vehicle? expand_more
For non-geared scooties (110cc-125cc), the minimum age is 18 years with a valid driving license. For performance motorcycles and four-wheeler passenger cars, the minimum age is 21 years with at least 1 year of driving experience.
Can I book a vehicle for someone else? expand_more
The person who performs the KYC and signs the digital undertaking must be present at the time of vehicle delivery to inspect and receive the vehicle keys.

account_balance_wallet Security Deposit & Refunds

When is my security deposit refunded? expand_more
DriveX processes automated security deposit refunds within 2 hours of vehicle drop-off audit. The amount is credited directly back to your original UPI ID or bank account.
Are there any hidden fuel or cancellation charges? expand_more
No hidden fees! All tariffs are transparent and inclusive of standard insurance. Cancellations made up to 6 hours before the rental start time receive a 100% full refund.

local_shipping Doorstep Delivery & Pickup

How does Doorstep Delivery work? expand_more
Select "Doorstep Delivery" during checkout and enter your exact address (Home, Office, Airport, Hotel). A trained DriveX fleet executive delivers the sanitized vehicle directly to your doorstep within your chosen delivery window.
What happens if I return the vehicle late? expand_more
A grace period of 30 minutes is allowed. Late returns beyond 30 minutes are charged at standard hourly rates plus nominal late fee to accommodate the next scheduled reservation.

storefront Franchise & Business Partnerships

How can I apply for a DriveX Franchise? expand_more
Visit our Own a Franchise page and click "Apply for Franchise" to open the official Google Form application. Our team will review your application and contact you within 24 hours.
